Geoimport

Imports photos off a camera card and places them on the GPS tracks your phone recorded. It runs as an Android app, and on Windows, macOS and Linux from the command line.

What it does

  • Records GPS tracks on your phone while you take photos with a camera that has no GPS of its own.
  • Imports new photos from the camera's memory card, picking up after the last photo it imported. You choose which file types to take, such as RAW, JPEG or video.
  • Adds the position to each photo from the track that covers the time it was taken. RAF, JPEG and MOV/MP4 get the position in the file itself; other formats get an XMP sidecar file.
  • Syncs between your devices if you want it to. Record on your phone, import on your tablet: tracks and import settings are kept in a folder in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive

Sync is optional and off until you sign in. The app only asks for access to the files it creates itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive, and the data never passes through a server of ours. There is none. See the privacy policy for the details.
