Geoimport

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Geoimport keeps your data on your own devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. There is no Geoimport server. Nothing is collected, sold or shared, and there are no ads or analytics.

What the app handles

Location

The app uses your location only while you record a track, and to show where you are on the map. A track is written as a GPX file to Documents/Geoimport on your device. It stays there until you delete it, and leaves the device only if you share it yourself or turn on sync.

Photos and videos

When you import, the app reads photos and videos from your camera's memory card, copies them to your device, and writes the position from your tracks into them. Photos and videos are never uploaded anywhere.

Settings

Import settings, such as your camera's clock offset, are kept on the device. With sync turned on, they are also kept in your Google Drive.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off until you sign in with a Google account in the app. It lets your tracks and import settings follow you between your own devices.

  • What the app can access: it asks only for permission to use files it creates itself. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • What it stores there: a Geoimport folder holding
    • your recorded tracks;
    • which photo your last import stopped at, and on which device;
    • your import settings: the file types to leave on the card, the camera clock offset and time zone, and how far from a track a photo may be placed.
  • Your account: the app reads your Google account's email address from Drive, only to show which account sync uses. The address is stored on your device and nowhere else.
  • Sign-in: signing in and access tokens are handled by Google Play services on your device. The app does not see or store your password.

The data travels directly between your device and Google Drive, over an encrypted connection. The developer has no access to it.

Geoimport's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

  • Map: map images are loaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, it receives your IP address and which part of the map is being shown. It does not receive your tracks or photos.
  • Device backup: if Android backup is on, Android may include the app's settings in your device backup, stored by Google in your account.

Removing your data

  • Stop syncing: in the app, open the menu and choose Sync tracks → Stop syncing. Files stay where they are.
  • Withdraw access: remove Geoimport's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete the synced data: delete the Geoimport folder in your Google Drive.
  • Delete data on a device: uninstalling the app removes its settings. Tracks in Documents/Geoimport and imported photos stay until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
